## 1. Purpose

Every module spec written so far says that someone is "notified" and leaves how to this document (ROADMAP §22; 05.13 §15: "templates and channels are its concern"). This spec covers:

- **The catalogue.** Every notification named in 05.1–05.8, 05.13 and the invoicing work (05.5 §7.3, 02 §21), each with a stable key, trigger, recipients, category and attachment.
- **One dispatch path.** Domain services raise events. They never send mail themselves.
- **Two tables.** `notification_preferences` records marketing consent with proof. `notification_log` records every message queued, with its delivery outcome.
- **Categories.** Transactional messages cannot be opted out of. Marketing messages require consent, and one-click unsubscribe honours its withdrawal immediately (07 §7.1, §7.4).
- **Delivery tracking, retry and suppression.**
- **Attachments.** The invoice PDF archived at issue, the quote PDF and the credit note PDF.

Out of scope: marketing automation (campaigns, segments, scheduling), which 02's scope statement defers to a module spec that does not exist yet; outbound API webhooks (06 §11, Phase 4), which are a machine-to-machine channel with their own contract; in-page messages rendered in the UI (§6.3).

## 4. Categories

Every notification belongs to exactly one category, fixed in code, not per send.

| Category | Legal basis (07 §7.1) | Opt-out | Unsubscribe header | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `transactional` | Contract | **No** — a message the service needs in order to work | No | Order confirmation, invoice, dispatch, quote sent, RMA approved |
| `security` | Contract / legitimate interest | **No**, and still sent when the address is marketing-suppressed | No | Password changed, 2FA changed, email changed |
| `marketing` | Consent, captured separately | **Yes**, opt-in by default | **Yes**, RFC 8058 one-click (§11.3) | None named in any existing spec (Q2) |

Rules:

- **Opt-in, never bundled.** Marketing consent is its own control. It is never a condition of registering, and never pre-ticked (07 §7.1: "Opt-in, not bundled with account creation").
- **Transactional means transactional.** A transactional template carries no promotional content. Adding a "you might also like" block makes the message marketing, and the whole message is then subject to consent. Template review enforces this (§13).
- **`security` is separate from `transactional`** so it can be exempt from marketing suppression and always go to the old address on an email change (05.13 §11).
- **Back-in-stock** (§5.6) is categorised `transactional`. The customer asked for this one message about this one SKU. Its stop link cancels the subscription instead of changing marketing consent. **Confirmed 2026-09-25 (Q3), for guests too:** one message, then the subscription closes (`status = 'notified'`).

#### 5.1.1 Avoiding duplicate messages for card orders

A card order is invoiced (or receipted) at capture (05.5 §7.3), seconds after placement. Sending `order.confirmed`, `payment.received` and `invoice.issued` for one checkout would triple-message the customer. **Approved 2026-09-25 (Q5):**

- A card order gets `order.confirmed` and then `invoice.issued`. The receipt or invoice **is** the payment acknowledgement.
- `payment.received` is sent for a payment applied to an invoice on any order that is not a card order: a BACS remittance, or a payment on account.

#### 5.1.2 Credit limit warning

- **Usage** is `credit_used_minor + credit_held_minor`, the exposure 05.2 §8 subtracts from the limit. The warning fires when a write takes usage from below 80% of `credit_limit_minor` to 80% or above, compared in integers: `usage × 100 ≥ limit × 80`. A company with a zero limit is never warned.
- **Once per breach.** It is re-armed only when usage falls back below 80%. Detection is at the crossing itself — the before and after values of the write that moved usage — so no "already warned" flag is stored. The dedup occurrence (§8.2) is the subject that caused the crossing (the order whose hold crossed it), so a retried event still sends one message.
- The writes that can cross upward are a new credit hold at checkout and a lowered limit. Invoice conversion moves a hold into `credit_used_minor` for roughly the same amount. It is checked too, since an invoice total can exceed its hold. Limit editing has no code path yet (05.2 admin tooling), and wires in when it does.
- Recipients are the company's `owner`s, not the default contact: the warning is about the account's buying authority, which owners manage (05.2 §10).

## 6. Channels

### 6.1 Email — launch

The only channel at launch. Sent through Laravel's mail layer and **Postmark** (Q12, decided 2026-09-25; CLAUDE.md approved stack; 07 §7.5 processor register). Postmark provides:

- delivery, bounce and complaint webhooks (§10.2);
- a message id returned on acceptance;
- RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers passed through untouched;
- a processing location recorded in the processor register (07 §7.5).

Transactional and security mail goes through Postmark's transactional message stream. Marketing, if the catalogue ever gains any, must use a separate broadcast stream, so complaints on one never affect the deliverability of the other. Postmark authenticates its webhooks by HTTP basic auth on the configured URL (HTTPS only), not by a signature. The credentials are configuration, compared in constant time.

Every email has an HTML part and a plain-text part, and meets 07 §8 accessibility for its HTML (semantic headings, alt text, sufficient contrast, no information conveyed by colour alone).

### 6.2 SMS — later

Modelled now so that adding it needs no schema change: `channel` is a column on both tables (§8), and `users.phone` already exists (02 §4.2). Not built at launch. Before it is:

- choose a provider (Q12);
- confirm which notifications are worth an SMS — dispatch and collection are the obvious candidates (Q13);
- capture SMS marketing consent separately from email: consent is per channel (§8.1);
- establish that transactional SMS needs a verified phone number. `users.phone` has no verification column (Q13).

### 6.3 Not a notification channel

- **In-page messages.** 05.2 §11 says `credit.limit_reached` is "in-app" for the buyer. That is the checkout page's own error response, not a notification, and is not logged here.
- **Real-time events.** The Socket.io gateway (11) pushes state changes to open pages, is fire-and-forget (Pub/Sub, 11 §5) and holds no domain data. It is not a delivery channel for anything that must arrive. A persistent in-app inbox is not specified (Q14).
- **API webhooks** (06 §11): machine-to-machine, own contract, Phase 4.

## 7. Recipients

### 7.1 Resolution

Resolved when the message is queued and recorded on the log row. A later change of email address does not re-route a message already sent.

| Scope | Recipients |
|---|---|
| User (auth, invitations) | That user's `users.email`, or the invitee's address |
| Order (trade) | The customer user on the order (`orders.user_id`) — never the rep who placed it on their behalf (`placed_by_user_id`, 05.8 §8) |
| Order (public) | `orders.user_id`'s email |
| Invoice messages (trade) — "invoice recipients": `invoice.*`, `payment.received` | **`companies.accounts_email` when set** (Q15, 02 §22). Otherwise the company's default contact (`company_users.is_default_contact`) plus every `owner` (05.2 §10: owners see all invoices), de-duplicated |
| Credit and account notices (trade): `credit.limit_changed`, `account.*` | Default contact plus every `owner`. The accounts mailbox handles paying, not buying authority |
| Invoice (public receipt) | `orders.user_id`'s email |
| Quote | The company user it was sent to, or `quotes.contact_email` when there is no account (05.3 §5) |
| Back-in-stock | `back_in_stock_subscriptions.email`, captured at signup (02 §14.10) |
| Staff role (`accounts`, `sales_manager`, `admin`) | Every active user holding the role (02 §14.1). See Q6 on shared team addresses |
| Rep | `companies.assigned_rep_user_id`; for quotes, `quotes.owner_user_id` |

**Accounts-payable address.** Wholesale customers routinely want invoices sent to a finance mailbox (`accounts@customer`) that belongs to no user. `companies.accounts_email` (02 §22) holds it. When set, it **replaces** the default contact and owners for invoice messages, rather than adding to them, so an AP team isn't copied on its own buyers' inboxes. A message to it has `user_id` NULL and `company_id` set.

### 7.2 Who never receives what

- **Cost and margin never reach a customer-facing message** (invariant 9). Customer templates are passed payloads with no cost fields at all — the same reasoning as the dropship packing note (05.8 §5.5): a template cannot render what it was never passed.
- **The end customer of a dropship order receives nothing from us** (05.8 §5.6: "we have no relationship with them"). The trade customer receives the tracking notification and forwards it.
- A `viewer` receives no order or invoice messages by virtue of the role alone. Viewers are recipients only as the default contact or as an order's customer user.

## 8. Schema

Two new tables, signed off 2026-09-25 and carried into 02 §22, the authoritative DDL.

### 8.1 `notification_preferences` — marketing consent, append-only

```sql
CREATE TABLE notification_preferences (
  id                    bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY,
  user_id               bigint      NOT NULL REFERENCES users (id),
  category              text        NOT NULL,
  channel               text        NOT NULL,
  opted_in              boolean     NOT NULL,
  source                text        NOT NULL,
  consent_text_version  text,
  ip                    inet,
  user_agent            text,
  actor_user_id         bigint      REFERENCES users (id),
  recorded_at           timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),

  CONSTRAINT notification_preferences_category_chk CHECK (category IN ('marketing')),
  CONSTRAINT notification_preferences_channel_chk  CHECK (channel IN ('email','sms')),
  CONSTRAINT notification_preferences_source_chk   CHECK (source IN
    ('registration','account_settings','unsubscribe_link','admin','erasure','complaint')),
  CONSTRAINT notification_preferences_consent_chk  CHECK (
    NOT opted_in OR consent_text_version IS NOT NULL
  )
);

CREATE INDEX notification_preferences_current_idx
  ON notification_preferences (user_id, category, channel, recorded_at DESC)
  INCLUDE (opted_in);
```

**Notes**

- **Append-only.** No `UPDATE`, no `DELETE` (except retention, §14). Every change of mind is a new row, and the **current state is the latest row** per `(user_id, category, channel)`. This is the only way to keep the proof of consent 07 §7.2 requires ("Marketing consent records: duration of consent + 3 years — proof of consent"). An updated-in-place row would overwrite the evidence the moment someone re-subscribed. It follows the same discipline as `stock_movements` and `payment_allocations` (§14.5.4).
- **No row means no consent.** Opt-in is the default (07 §7.1), so a user with no rows receives no marketing. No rows are seeded.
- **The proof** is the opt-in row itself: `recorded_at` (the timestamp), `source` (where it was given), `consent_text_version` (exactly what wording the user agreed to), plus `ip` and `user_agent`. `notification_preferences_consent_chk` makes an opt-in without its wording impossible to persist.
- **`category` is a one-value closed list.** Only marketing is consent-based. Transactional and security messages cannot be opted out of, so they have no preference rows. Storing a preference that is always ignored would suggest to a reader that it does something. If an adjustable category is ever wanted (for example, muting optional reminders), it is added to this list through §2.5's `NOT VALID`/`VALIDATE` sequence.
- **Consent is per channel.** Email consent is not SMS consent.
- **`user_id` is `NOT NULL`.** Only account holders can consent to marketing here. No guest marketing signup is specified anywhere (Q16).
- `actor_user_id` is set when staff record a change on a customer's behalf (`source = 'admin'`) — for example, a customer who asked by phone. It is NULL when the user acted themselves.
- `source = 'complaint'`: a spam complaint from the provider (§10.2) opts the user out automatically.
- `notification_preferences_current_idx` resolves "may we market to this user on this channel" as one index-only scan (latest row via `ORDER BY recorded_at DESC LIMIT 1`), per 02 §9 rule 2.

### 8.2 `notification_log` — every message, with its outcome

```sql
CREATE TABLE notification_log (
  id                   bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY,
  notification_key     text        NOT NULL,
  category             text        NOT NULL,
  channel              text        NOT NULL,
  template_version     text        NOT NULL,
  user_id              bigint      REFERENCES users (id),
  company_id           bigint      REFERENCES companies (id),
  recipient            text        NOT NULL,
  subject_type         text,
  subject_id           bigint,
  attachment_id        bigint      REFERENCES attachments (id),
  dedup_key            text        NOT NULL,
  status               text        NOT NULL DEFAULT 'queued',
  attempts             smallint    NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  provider_message_id  text,
  last_error           text,
  queued_at            timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
  sent_at              timestamptz,
  delivered_at         timestamptz,
  failed_at            timestamptz,
  updated_at           timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),

  CONSTRAINT notification_log_dedup_uq UNIQUE (dedup_key),
  CONSTRAINT notification_log_category_chk CHECK (category IN ('transactional','security','marketing')),
  CONSTRAINT notification_log_channel_chk  CHECK (channel IN ('email','sms')),
  CONSTRAINT notification_log_status_chk   CHECK (status IN
    ('queued','sent','delivered','bounced','complained','failed','suppressed')),
  CONSTRAINT notification_log_subject_chk  CHECK ((subject_type IS NULL) = (subject_id IS NULL))
);

CREATE UNIQUE INDEX notification_log_provider_uq
  ON notification_log (channel, provider_message_id) WHERE provider_message_id IS NOT NULL;
CREATE INDEX notification_log_subject_idx
  ON notification_log (subject_type, subject_id, queued_at DESC) WHERE subject_type IS NOT NULL;
CREATE INDEX notification_log_user_idx
  ON notification_log (user_id, queued_at DESC) WHERE user_id IS NOT NULL;
CREATE INDEX notification_log_suppression_idx
  ON notification_log (channel, recipient) WHERE status IN ('bounced','complained');
CREATE INDEX notification_log_pending_idx
  ON notification_log (queued_at) WHERE status = 'queued';
```

**Notes**

- **One row per message per recipient**, written *before* sending (§9). It is the record of truth for "was it sent". An email that never got a row was never sent, and a row stuck at `queued` is visible (§10.3).
- **`dedup_key` makes dispatch idempotent.** Domain events are delivered at least once: after-commit listeners can run twice on a retried job, and the invoicing triggers are idempotent by design (02 §21). The key is `{notification_key}:{subject_type}:{subject_id}:{recipient}:{occurrence}`, where `occurrence` is empty for one-off messages and the date for reminders (`invoice.due_soon`, `order.awaiting_approval_reminder`). A second insert fails on `notification_log_dedup_uq` and is treated as "already queued". It never produces a second email.
- **`recipient` is the address at queue time**, deliberately copied. The user's current email may differ later, and "we sent it to the address you had then" is the answer to a dispute. It is personal data and anonymised on erasure (§14).
- **`user_id` is nullable** for recipients with no account: a quote `contact_email`, a guest back-in-stock subscriber, an applicant before approval.
- **`subject_type`/`subject_id` are polymorphic with no FK**, like `attachments` (02 §14.2): the subject spans unrelated tables, several of which (`quotes`, `rmas`, `shipments`) are not migrated yet. Not a closed `CHECK` list for now (Q17).
- **`attachment_id` points at the archived document** (02 §21.1) and never at a copy. The customer's attachment and the archive are the same bytes by construction. A single nullable column is enough: no catalogued notification carries more than one attachment (§12).
- **`template_version`** records which template revision rendered the message. **The rendered body is not stored**: it would duplicate personal and financial data already held in its source records, with no retention basis of its own. To reconstruct a message, render `template_version` against the subject.
- **`status` is the latest outcome**, not a history. The transitions are few and provider events arrive in order per message. A full event history is not needed for any stated requirement.
- `notification_log_suppression_idx` answers "has this address hard-bounced or complained" at queue time (§10.4) without a separate suppression table (Q18).
- `notification_log_pending_idx` drives the stuck-message check (§10.3).
- **Not partitioned.** Volume is roughly one to four rows per order plus reminders, well below `stock_movements`' growth (02 §10). Revisit on measured size.

## 9. Dispatch pipeline

```
domain transaction commits
  └─ DB::afterCommit → domain event (InvoiceIssued, OrderPlaced, ShipmentDispatched…)
        └─ listener → NotificationDispatcher::send(key, subject, context)
              1. resolve recipients (§7.1)
              2. per recipient:
                   a. marketing? → consent check (§8.1); no consent → no row
                   b. suppression check (§10.4) → row at 'suppressed', stop
                   c. INSERT notification_log (queued), ON CONFLICT (dedup_key) DO NOTHING
                   d. if inserted → dispatch SendNotification job (log id)
        SendNotification job
              render template → send via provider → status 'sent',
              provider_message_id, sent_at, attempts++
```

- **Nothing is sent inside a domain transaction** (CLAUDE.md invariant 6; 04 §4.4: "Side effects… are queued jobs dispatched after commit"). A rolled-back order sends nothing, and a slow provider never holds a stock or credit lock.
- **`NotificationDispatcher` is the only path** (ROADMAP §22). No domain service builds or sends a Mailable itself. Existing code that should route through it: `OrderPlaced`, `PaymentCaptured`, `InvoiceIssued` (02 §21) and Laravel's password-reset and verification notifications. The last two are replaced by catalogue entries so they are logged like everything else.
- **Mail down: queued, not lost** (07 §4). Jobs wait on the queue and orders complete regardless.
- **Consent is checked at queue time and again at send time** for marketing. An unsubscribe between queueing and sending is honoured (07 §7.4: "honoured immediately"). The row moves to `suppressed` with `last_error = 'consent withdrawn'`.

## 10. Delivery tracking and retry

### 10.1 Status lifecycle

```
queued ──send ok──▶ sent ──provider: delivered──▶ delivered
  │                   ├──provider: hard bounce──▶ bounced
  │                   └──provider: complaint────▶ complained   (may follow delivered)
  ├──transient failure──▶ queued (attempts++, backoff)
  ├──attempts exhausted / permanent reject──▶ failed
  └──suppressed at queue or send time──▶ suppressed
```

### 10.2 Provider webhooks

- `POST /api/v1/webhooks/postmark` receives Postmark's delivery, bounce and spam-complaint events. Its basic-auth credentials (§6.1) are checked before anything is parsed, the same posture as the Stripe webhook's signature.
- The row is matched on `(channel, provider_message_id)`, `notification_log_provider_uq`. Unknown ids are acknowledged and ignored.
- **Idempotent.** Replayed events leave the row where it is. A later-state event never moves a row back (`complained` is not overwritten by a late `delivered`).
- **Soft bounces** (mailbox full, greylisting) are the provider's retry concern and do not change status. **Hard bounces** set `bounced`. **Complaints** set `complained`, and for a user, write a `notification_preferences` opt-out (`source = 'complaint'`) for every marketing channel.

### 10.3 Retry

- Transient failures (provider 5xx, timeout, rate limit) are retried with exponential backoff **for 24 hours**, matching 06 §11's webhook retry window. After that the row is `failed`.
- Permanent rejections (invalid address syntax, provider 4xx other than rate limiting) go straight to `failed`.
- **A failed or bounced transactional message on a money document** (`invoice.issued`, `invoice.overdue`, `rma.resolved`) is surfaced to the `accounts` role. An invoice the customer never received must not age silently into an overdue dispute (U7). This is a staff list in the admin panel, not a recursive email about an email (Q19).
- A row at `queued` older than a threshold with no job running is reported. This is the notifications case of 07 §9.3's P2 "queue age exceeding threshold".

### 10.4 Suppression

| Last outcome for `(channel, recipient)` | `marketing` | `transactional` | `security` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bounced` (hard) | Suppressed | Suppressed, surfaced per §10.3 | **Attempted** |
| `complained` | Suppressed | Sent | Sent |

A hard-bounced address stays suppressed until the user changes or re-verifies their email. Re-verification is itself `transactional`, so it is attempted once. Security notices are always attempted: a message about a possible account takeover is worth a second bounce.

## 11. Consent and one-click unsubscribe

### 11.1 Capturing consent

- A separate, unticked checkbox wherever marketing is offered (registration, 05.13 §5; account settings). Never a condition of anything (07 §7.1).
- The wording shown is versioned. Its version string is stored on the opt-in row (`consent_text_version`).
- Staff may record consent given by phone (`source = 'admin'`, `actor_user_id` set) — to be confirmed as acceptable evidence (Q20).

### 11.2 The proof

A regulator or a complaint is answered from `notification_preferences` alone: *on `recorded_at`, from `ip`/`user_agent`, via `source`, the user agreed to `consent_text_version`*, followed by every later change in order. This is the "proof of consent" 07 §7.2 retains for the duration of consent plus 3 years.

Note on references: this spec's brief cites 07 §7.2 for one-click unsubscribe. In 07, one-click unsubscribe is §7.4 ("Objection to marketing — one-click unsubscribe, honoured immediately"). §7.2 is the retention of consent records. Both apply and both are satisfied here.

### 11.3 One-click unsubscribe

Every `marketing` email carries:

- `List-Unsubscribe: <https://…/unsubscribe/{token}>, <mailto:…>`
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058)
- a visible unsubscribe link in the body.

The **token** is a signed, non-expiring value naming the user, category and channel. It needs no sign-in (07 §7.4). It is not a session and grants nothing else.

| Request | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `POST /unsubscribe/{token}` (the mail client's one-click) | Writes an opt-out row (`source = 'unsubscribe_link'`, `recorded_at = now()`) and returns 200. Honoured immediately, including for messages already queued (§9) |
| `GET /unsubscribe/{token}` (a person clicking the link) | Shows a confirmation page with one button that performs the POST. A GET never changes state on its own, because mail scanners prefetch links and would otherwise unsubscribe people who never clicked |
| Already opted out | Same success response, no new row |
| Token invalid | A neutral page offering account settings. It never reveals whether an address exists |

**Transactional and security emails carry no `List-Unsubscribe` header**, since there is nothing to unsubscribe from. Back-in-stock carries a "stop this alert" link that cancels that subscription (02 §14.10 `status = 'cancelled'`) and touches no marketing consent.

## 12. Attachments

### 12.1 Which notifications carry one

| Notification | Attachment | Archived where | Status of the archive |
|---|---|---|---|
| `invoice.issued` | Invoice PDF, or receipt PDF for a public order | `attachments`, `attachable_type = 'invoice'` (02 §21.1) | Built. The PDF is produced only once a renderer exists (docs/12-pdf-worker.md) |
| `quote.sent` | Quote PDF with accept/decline links | `attachments`, `attachable_type = 'quote'` (02 §22, Q7) | Type allowed; archiving lands with quotes (05.3) |
| `rma.resolved` | Credit note PDF | `attachments`, `attachable_type = 'credit_note'` (02 §22, Q7) | Type allowed; archiving lands with credit notes (05.4) |
| `rma.approved` | Return label, when one is provided | Not specified (05.4 §7.2 says only "whether a label is provided") | Gap (Q9) |

No other catalogued notification carries an attachment. Dispatch messages carry a tracking link, not a document. Packing notes and pick lists are warehouse documents, never emailed.

### 12.2 Rules

- **The attachment is the archive.** `notification_log.attachment_id` references the archived file. The email never carries a separately rendered copy, so what the customer received and what the business holds are the same bytes (02 §21.1).
- **`invoice.issued` is sent at issue** (Q21, decided 2026-09-25). It carries the document number, issue date, due date (invoices), net, VAT and total, and a link to the order in the customer portal, where the document can be read. While `NullPdfRenderer` is bound, nothing is attached.
- **The PDF is attached when the worker exists.** At send time, the archived PDF is attached if it exists. When a real renderer is bound (docs/12-pdf-worker.md), the `invoice.issued` trigger moves from `InvoiceIssued` to archive completion, so the PDF is always attached. The move is one line in `InvoiceService`, part of the worker's delivery.
- The portal link today is the order page (`/orders/{id}/confirmation`), the only customer order page. It becomes the order's documents page (06 §8, `/orders/{id}/documents`) when that is built.
- **Size.** Attachments above a provider limit (typically 10 MB, far above any expected invoice) are sent as a signed, expiring download link instead (07 §6.3: served through the application, never a public storage URL).
- A missing archive is never regenerated on the fly for an email.

## 13. Templates

- **Blade, for mail only** (Q22, decided 2026-09-25; CLAUDE.md). Two shared views, `resources/views/mail/notification.blade.php` (HTML) and `notification-text.blade.php` (plain text), render a structured content object: subject, heading, paragraphs, label/value facts, one optional action button, and closing lines. Each notification key has its own content builder class that fills that object from an explicit payload. Keeping the markup shared means one accessible, tested layout (07 §8), and a key's wording lives in one class.
- Each key carries a `template_version`, recorded on the log and bumped whenever its wording changes.
- **Payloads are explicit and minimal.** A template receives a data array built for it, never a model, so it cannot reach a cost field or a relation it was not given (§7.2).
- **Money is formatted server-side** next to its integer (06 §3.1), using the same integer-only formatter as documents. There are no floats (invariant 1).
- Quantities in packs with the base-unit equivalent (invariant 2), as on invoices.
- **Locale `en_GB` only** at launch (07 §13), dates in the customer's local UK time.
- Every template change is reviewed against §4: a transactional template gaining promotional content changes its category.

## 14. Retention and erasure

| Data | Period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| `notification_preferences` | Duration of consent + 3 years, then purged | 07 §7.2 ("Marketing consent records") |
| `notification_log` | **2 years**, then rows deleted, for every category and subject (Q23, decided 2026-09-25) | 07 §7.2 as amended |

**Erasure** (07 §7.3, anonymisation, not deletion):

- `notification_log.recipient` is overwritten with the same tombstone used for `users.email`. The row survives, so "invoice INV-… was delivered on …" still stands for the retained invoice.
- `notification_preferences`: an opt-out row (`source = 'erasure'`) is written for every channel, and marketing stops at once. Earlier rows are **retained for the consent-proof period** under 07 §7.2, but with the user tombstoned, they no longer identify a person by email. Whether tombstoned proof is still adequate proof is a legal question (Q24).
- Back-in-stock subscriptions for the erased email are cancelled.

## 16. Edge cases

| Case | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Domain event delivered twice | Second `INSERT` hits `notification_log_dedup_uq`; one email |
| Unsubscribe between queue and send | Honoured at send time; row `suppressed` (§9) |
| User changes email after a message is queued | The queued message goes to the recorded `recipient`. Security notice to the old address per 05.13 §11 |
| Order placed by a rep on behalf of a customer | Customer user notified, not the rep (§7.1) |
| Company has no default contact and no active owner | Invoice notification has no recipient: logged with an alert to `accounts`, never silently dropped (Q15) |
| Invoice email hard-bounces | Row `bounced`; surfaced to `accounts` (§10.3); further transactional mail to that address suppressed until re-verified |
| Public customer erased after purchase | Receipt row's `recipient` tombstoned; receipt retained 7 years (07 §7.2) |
| Back-in-stock for a SKU that sells out again before the email is sent | Sent anyway. The message says stock returned, not that it is still available, and links to the live product page |
| Quote sent to a `contact_email` that later registers | Past messages keep `user_id` NULL; new ones resolve to the user |
| Invoice issued with no PDF renderer configured | Invoice email sent without an attachment, with number, totals and portal link (§12.2) |
| Marketing email to a user suspended or closed | Not sent: closed and suspended users receive transactional mail about their account only |
| Provider webhook for a message id we never recorded | Acknowledged, ignored, logged at debug |

## 17. Test matrix

**Dispatch:** nothing sent when the domain transaction rolls back · one log row per recipient per event, even when the event fires twice · reminder dedup is per day, so a second daily run sends nothing · `NotificationDispatcher` is the only caller of the mailer (architecture test: no Mailable sent outside it).

**Categories and consent:** a user with no preference rows receives no marketing · an opt-in without `consent_text_version` is rejected by the database · the latest row wins after in → out → in · transactional and security messages ignore preferences · a transactional template with promotional content fails template review tests (a lint on template payload keys).

**Unsubscribe:** RFC 8058 POST opts out with no session and writes a row with `recorded_at` · GET changes nothing until the button is pressed · a tampered token is rejected with a neutral page · transactional mail has no `List-Unsubscribe` header · back-in-stock stop link cancels the subscription and leaves marketing consent unchanged.

**Delivery:** webhook credentials verified before parsing · replayed webhook leaves status unchanged · a late `delivered` does not overwrite `complained` · complaint writes a marketing opt-out · transient failure retried with backoff, `failed` after 24 h · hard bounce suppresses marketing and transactional but security is still attempted.

**Attachments:** `invoice.issued` is sent at issue with number, totals and portal link · when an archive exists at send time, the attached bytes equal the archived file · a customer-facing payload contains no cost field (response inspection, as 05.3 §14).

**Recipients:** order-on-behalf notifies the customer, not the rep · invoice recipients are the default contact plus owners, de-duplicated · the dropship end customer never appears as a recipient.

**Retention and erasure:** erasure tombstones `recipient` and writes opt-outs · consent rows survive erasure · log retention job deletes only past-period, non-financial rows.

## 18. Acceptance criteria

1. Every notification in §5 without a ⚑ is sent through `NotificationDispatcher` and logged in `notification_log`.
2. No email is sent from inside a domain transaction, and a rolled-back transaction sends nothing.
3. A repeated domain event never produces a second email (`dedup_key`).
4. No user receives marketing without an opt-in row carrying its consent wording version and timestamp.
5. Every marketing email supports RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe without sign-in, honoured immediately, including for already-queued messages.
6. Transactional and security messages cannot be opted out of and carry no unsubscribe header.
7. An administrator can show, for any invoice, whether and when its email was sent and delivered.
8. A bounced invoice email is visible to accounts staff.
9. When an invoice email carries a PDF, it is the archived PDF (02 §21.1), byte for byte.
10. No customer-facing message contains cost or margin.
11. Consent records are retained for the duration of consent plus 3 years and are append-only.
