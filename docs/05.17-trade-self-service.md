## 1. Scope and existing foundation

Complete trade dashboard, company order history/details, invoices, credit notes,
statements and applicant status/reply. The existing public `/account/orders` and
receipt pages use public-user policies; do not expose them as company trade history.
Existing invoicing/attachments are useful foundations, but
`app/Providers/AppServiceProvider.php` currently binds `PdfRenderer` to
`app/Domain/Documents/NullPdfRenderer.php`, which returns null. Issued documents are
not evidence of a real archived PDF. The referenced `docs/12-pdf-worker.md` does not
exist. 01 §5.1 mentions Puppeteer, but CLAUDE.md's approved stack names no PDF library:
§5 below is a **choice proposal**, not approval inferred from that older mention.

All screens comply with [05.16](05.16-b2b-ux-standard.md): shared trade shell/header,
mobile cards, four designed states, confirmation, screenshots/axe, 07 budgets. Applicant
pages use the storefront/auth shell until a company membership exists; no empty trade
sidebar or financial access. Staff financial administration remains Filament.

## 2. Rules, financial visibility and payloads

Every trade request resolves active company from verified session membership and
rechecks it; ignore customer-supplied company_id. Switching company resets filters,
cursors, cart context and server-state cache. Orders are company-scoped; owner/buyer/
approver/viewer can read company orders, but only buying roles may reorder/request
returns. Proposed invoice/credit note/statement/balance access is owner/approver plus
accounts/admin; users lacking finance permission receive only relevant order totals,
not account-wide balances/downloads. Assigned rep access is a separate explicit staff
policy, with customer data DTOs still excluding costs. Apply policy at GET, polling,
PDF request and download, not only navigation.

Dashboard: current account status and terms, available credit (with over-limit warning),
spendable balance for financial roles, pending approvals when eligible, recent orders,
active quotes and quick reorder/saved-list links. Clearly distinguish pending payment,
awaiting approval, stock-not-reserved and dispatched. Invoice due/overdue totals derive
from gross − cash paid − credited (05.2 §18); balance never increases credit limit.

History uses keyset `(placed_at DESC,id DESC)` and invoice/credit-note history
`(issued_at DESC,id DESC)`, 50 default/100 maximum. Nullable draft placed_at is excluded
from order history and presented separately as a draft/approval queue. Sort whitelist
supports date ascending/descending (same index backward scan) and stable tie-breaker;
filters number/customer-reference/status/date range. Do not add unindexed arbitrary
financial amount sorting. Search/date filters bind cursor to company/filter/sort.
Bulk download only selected loaded documents; queue a private bundle, recheck policies
on each entry and explain unavailable PDFs before confirmation. ZIP is not a PDF library.

Detail reads immutable order/line, shipment and invoice snapshots; no price re-resolution
on history and no live company-address substitution into issued PDFs. Existing order
cancellation routes can be linked only when their signed-off policies permit trade;
reorder and returns use 05.1/05.4. Never expose cost, staff notes, raw storage keys or
accounting provider identifiers in customer DTOs/documents.

Statement is a fixed **as-of** export with from_on/to_on (UK calendar dates) and
cutoff_at captured at request. Opening debt, period invoices, cash allocations,
credit-note allocations, closing debt and ageing are shown separately from opening/
period/closing spendable balance movements and uninvoiced credit holds. A credit note
allocated to an invoice must not be counted again as extra spendable balance. Use one
coherent database snapshot to materialise the payload (single SQL snapshot or ordered
company-lock capture at READ COMMITTED), then render outside the transaction. Persist
as account_statements + immutable document_renders payload; later postings do not
change the archived statement. UTC timestamps remain machine format, display dates
and expiry in Europe/London/en-GB. Default range current month, max 12 months per
request; older history remains available via additional bounded requests.

Applicant status belongs solely to applicant_user_id (and existing scoped email-token
flow, where configured). Show submitted/in_review/info_requested/approved/rejected/
withdrawn with dates and next step. Display customer-safe info_request/rejection message,
never review_note/HMRC evidence/internal reason; rejected shows reapply_after and
remediable outcome from 02 §25.2. Respond to info_requested with bounded text and
validated private evidence attachment; staff receives after-commit notification and
queue returns to in_review without erasing the previous audit. Withdrawal requires
confirmation and affects only an open application. Reapplication observes cooling
period/terms/legal-form checks in 05.2/05.13; do not promise checkout while pending.
Approval prompts company selection/reprice notice on next request, not a silent
migration of public cart into another company's basket.

## 3. Schema amendment and immutable documents

Full proposed SQL is [02 §31.2](02-domain-model-erd.md#312-self-service-and-pdfs-0517):
`document_renders`, `account_statements`, private attachment type additions and
company/date/id cursor indexes. Reuse orders/order_lines/shipments, invoices,
credit_notes, b2b_applications and attachments. No duplicate financial-line tables.
Render envelope: document_type, public reference, schema/template version, company,
issue/cutoff timestamp, immutable seller/customer/delivery identity, already-calculated
integer amounts **and** formatted display values, line snapshots and footer terms.
Source payload and asset hashes are fixed at issue/send; approved local assets only.
Statement envelope additionally records range, opening/closing figures and movement
rows; blind packing slip uses an independent whitelist with no monetary fields.

Document status pending → rendering → ready or failed; retry failed with same payload
and source/version key, never issue another invoice/note number. Jobs use existing
PdfDocument/PdfRenderer boundary, return real nonempty `%PDF-` bytes or a classified
failure; null renderer cannot count as successful production rendering. Archive bytes
on private storage and link attachment; validate MIME/size/page count and checksum.
Ready source/template/payload/attachment content is immutable. New layout/version
can render a new approved document version while original archive remains retrievable;
never change original financial content or issued identity. Job lease/queue retry
recovers a worker crash; lease is queue infrastructure, no extra invented domain column.

Before historical backfill, use existing issued snapshots or archived payload if
available. Missing seller/customer-at-issue evidence is a **backfill blocker**, not
permission to fabricate a historical invoice from today's profile. Accounts approves
an explicit marked reconstruction process separately if needed.

## 4. Screens and proposed 06 routes

All `{id}` values are public ULIDs. Inertia pages use verified session+active-company
middleware; `/api/v1` JSON mutations use CSRF, existing 06 error/idempotency envelopes,
company-scope policy and rate limiting. Draft route names reconcile with 06 on sign-off,
not a second parallel implementation. Private binary downloads are ordinary browser
GET routes, explicitly excluded from 06's JSON-only API convention.

| Screen | Layout and primary action | Routes / policy |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Account/terms/credit cards, overdue/approval alert, recent orders and quick tools; Start order primary | GET `/trade`; all trade members, financial cards redacted by role |
| Order history | Number/reference search, status/date chips, useful personal views; date-sort table/cards with totals; Start order primary | GET `/trade/orders`; GET `/api/v1/trade/orders`; all company members |

| Invoices list | Due/overdue summary, status/date filters, invoice/issued/due/paid/credited/outstanding table/cards; Export selected secondary | GET `/trade/invoices`; GET `/api/v1/trade/invoices`; owner/approver, accounts/admin |
| Invoice detail | Fixed issued identity and item/VAT totals; paid/credited allocation timeline; Download primary with preparing/failed states | GET `/trade/invoices/{id}`; GET `/trade/invoices/{id}/download`; same finance policy |
| Credit notes list/detail | Date/reason filters and note table/cards; detail shows original invoice, fees/VAT, allocated debt/excess balance; Download primary | GET `/trade/credit-notes`, `/trade/credit-notes/{id}`, `/trade/credit-notes/{id}/download`; finance policy |

Download GET checks membership/source policy every time then returns archived PDF or
short-lived (proposed 60 seconds) signed private-storage URL, Cache-Control private,
no-store. It **does not queue rendering**; when not ready, return 409 status and a
source-page link; the explicit POST queues. Bundle request proposed POST
`/api/v1/trade/document-bundles`, returns bounded existing queued-job handle/status
contract to be specified before optional bulk-bundle delivery; single-document PDFs
are the required first slice. No invented persistent bundle table is authorised here.

## 5. PDF renderer decision — A approved 2026-10-05

**Chosen: A — Puppeteer via `spatie/browsershot`, approved 2026-10-05 and added to
CLAUDE.md.** B/C are retained as rejected alternatives, not runtime options. No
package has been installed by the spec sign-off; delivery belongs to Module 2. Assessment is architectural judgement based on official
capabilities, **not measured repo performance or a claim of completed security review**.

| Option | Advantages | Trade-offs | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Puppeteer + pinned Chromium, isolated Node worker** ([API](https://pptr.dev/api/puppeteer.page.pdf)) | Modern print CSS, close match to storefront typography; fits existing Node capability and 01's earlier direction | Chromium image/memory/security maintenance; separate bounded worker and PHP→Node job protocol; cold start needs P25 measurement | Preferred production option if operating a browser worker is acceptable |
| **B. Playwright + pinned Chromium, isolated Node worker** ([PDF API](https://playwright.dev/docs/api/class-page#page-pdf)) | Browser PDF plus shared tooling knowledge with required screenshot acceptance; modern CSS | PDF uses Chromium, not all browser engines; renderer worker must be separate from test runner; similar operating cost to A | Choose if consolidating browser tooling matters more than the original Puppeteer direction |
| **C. Dompdf, queued PHP renderer** ([official project](https://github.com/dompdf/dompdf)) | PHP-only queue deployment; suitable simple text/table documents without a browser runtime | CSS limitations including no flexbox/grid; distinct print templates and careful long-table/page-break testing; may take more layout work | Suitable when smallest operational footprint outweighs visual parity |

Regardless of choice: renderer accepts trusted explicit payload, never arbitrary URL,
HTML, JavaScript or local filesystem path from a buyer. Blade **mail/PDF templates are
permitted by CLAUDE.md**; if chosen for print HTML they live in
`resources/views/documents/` and accept explicit arrays, not lazy ORM models. All browser
pages remain React/Inertia. Escape customer text; disable script execution and remote
network/file fetching, allow only packaged/validated fonts/assets; reject unsupported
asset types. Chromium runs sandboxed as unprivileged user with resource/time limits,
not `--no-sandbox`. PHP option restricts local paths/remote resources equivalently.
Limit concurrency and prewarm where appropriate; capture safe job metrics, not PII.
A4 templates repeat table headings, keep totals together and avoid clipped addresses;
embed approved fonts/logo and generate searchable text. Accessible HTML remains the
primary account view; PDF tagging/accessibility must be assessed for the chosen renderer.
Meet 07 P25 p95 **3 seconds queued generation** on representative 1/10/100-page fixtures;
queue wait and generation reported separately. No claim any option already meets it.

## 6. Required tests and open questions

Tests: every company/role/public/applicant cross-access including guessed IDs, render
polling/download after membership revocation and company switch; immutable amounts/
identity/template at reprint; missing historical snapshots blocked; out-of-order
invoice/cash/note events and separate debt/balance statement totals; as-of consistency
while payment posts; UTC/London date boundaries; keyset ties and filter reset; constant
queries and 07 P7; no source costs/internal notes in JSON/PDF; queued real bytes/archive,
retry/crash/source dedupe, failure/no null success, malicious strings/assets/URLs;
PDF extracted text and rendered page checks for A4 totals, long addresses, 100 pages,
blind-slip redaction; applicant reply/withdraw/reapply/cooling policies. Screens meet
05.16 Playwright/axe/manual focus gates. Real renderer performance is tested after
selection, not by substituting NullPdfRenderer.

| Open question | Recommendation / blocking dependency |
|---|---|
| Which renderer: A, B or C? | A approved: Puppeteer via spatie/browsershot; implement in Module 2. |
| Financial access for ordinary buyer/viewer? | Owner/approver initially; ordinary roles see company orders, not account-wide financial documents. |
| Statement range / PDF preparation? | Max 12-month window, fixed cutoff; asynchronous rendering with explicit prepare/retry and authorised private download. |
| Historical invoices missing issue identity? | Accounts-reviewed reconstruction separately; never silently use current profile. |
| Bulk PDF bundles / tagged PDFs? | Single PDFs first; bundle job persistence and tagged-PDF accessibility need renderer-specific follow-up before those optional features. |
