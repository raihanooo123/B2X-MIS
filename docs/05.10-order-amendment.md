## 1. Scope

ROADMAP §20 gives this document four jobs: amendment after a credit hold, the cancellation fee, the RMA→cancellation boundary, and cancelling part of an order. **This draft writes only the minimum for the last one: §2, cancelling some quantity of an order before it is dispatched.** It replaces two stop-gaps: "whole order only" (05.15 §12 Q6) and "staff handle part-dispatched orders" (Q7).

Not written yet, and not needed for §2: amending an order (adding lines, raising quantities, changing the address), re-running the credit check on an increase, cancellation fees (`orders.cancellation_fee_minor` stays 0), and approval of amendments.

## 2. Cancelling part of an order before dispatch — signed off 2026-10-05

### 2.1 What can be cancelled

- **Orders:** `status` in `confirmed`, `picking` or `part_dispatched`. An order in `awaiting_approval` or `pending_payment` is cancelled whole, as today. A `dispatched`, `completed` or `cancelled` order has nothing left to cancel. After dispatch, a consumer returns goods (05.4 §13.3) and trade goes through an RMA.
- **Quantities:** per line, whole packs of that line's pack, up to
  `cancellable_base_qty = base_qty − dispatched_base_qty − cancelled_base_qty − packed_base_qty`,
  where `packed_base_qty` is the line's quantity in a shipment with status `packed`. Packed goods are refused (409 `line_packed`) until staff unpack them. Quantity that is only allocated, or picked but not packed, can be cancelled.
- **Replacements** (05.4 §14) cannot be cancelled partially (05.4 Q-R4).
- **Who:**
  - **Consumer** (public or guest, `company_id IS NULL`): the customer, from the order page or through their guest link. Or staff, recording a cancellation the customer made by email or phone, with the time they told us (CCR reg. 32). This is a statutory right: free, and no reason needed.
  - **Trade:** the user who placed the order, or a company `owner` or `approver` (05.2 §10), or staff. Reason required.
- **Break quantity (decided Q-X1, 2026-10-05).** On a trade order, a **customer** may not leave a line below the quantity of the price break that priced it (`order_lines.applied_break_qty`). After the cancellation, the line's kept quantity (`base_qty − cancelled_base_qty`) must be either 0 (the whole line cancelled) or at least `applied_break_qty`. Anything else is refused with 422 `below_break_quantity`, stating the break quantity. **Staff** may cancel below it, with a reason: `reason_code = 'below_break_override'` and a non-blank `reason_detail`, audited as `order.cancel_below_break`. A line priced without a break (`applied_break_qty` NULL or 1) has no limit. **Consumers are not restricted**: cancelling any part of an order is a statutory right (CCR reg. 29), so a consumer's partial cancellation is never refused for this reason, and is never re-priced.
- **Everything left cancelled:** if a cancellation leaves no undispatched quantity on any line, then a not-yet-dispatched order becomes `cancelled` (the same end state as 05.4 §13.2), and a `part_dispatched` order becomes `dispatched`.

### 2.2 Money

**Prices are never re-resolved** (invariant 4). The kept lines keep their snapshotted prices. A trade customer can't drop a line below its break quantity (§2.1, Q-X1), and a spend break or carriage-paid threshold no longer met stays as placed (Q-X5). The delivery charge stands.

**The cancelled share of a line** uses the same cumulative rounding as per-shipment invoices (05.5 §7.3), so cancellations plus invoices of a line always sum to its total, to the penny. For a line with total T (net and tax independently, as 05.5 does), `base_qty` B and total cancelled quantity C:

```
billable_total(C) = T                                   if C = 0
                  = round_half_up(T × (B − C) / B)       otherwise
cancelled share   = billable_total(C_before) − billable_total(C_after)
```

Later per-shipment invoices bill `billable_total(C)` over `B − C` units instead of T over B. `ShipmentInvoiceShares` takes the cancelled quantity as an input. A line cancelled in full has a cancelled share of exactly what remained.

| Order is | Effect of the cancelled gross G |
|---|---|
| Card, captured | A `payments` refund row (`type = 'refund'`, `status = 'pending'`, `refunded_payment_id`) for G. The gateway refund runs after commit. The original payment becomes `part_refunded` |
| BACS paid / prepay paid | A pending refund row, `gateway = 'bacs'`, paid by accounts (as 05.4 §13.2) |
| Unpaid (BACS awaiting payment, pay at collection) | Nothing to refund. The amount due falls by G |
| Trade on account, not yet invoiced in full | The credit hold falls by G (05.2 §8.3 "order total changes before invoicing"), and `companies.credit_held_minor` with it. A hold reaching 0 is `released` |
| A receipt or prepaid invoice already covers the whole order | Credit note `reason = 'cancellation'` for G against it. A consumer receipt writes no account movement (02 §14.5.3, A8). Trade: Q-X2 |

**Consumer delivery refund:** none for a partial cancellation, because something is kept (05.15 §7.2, the same reading as Q9). If the cancellation leaves nothing kept and nothing was dispatched, the outbound delivery is refunded in full, exactly as 05.4 §13.2 does for a whole-order cancellation.

### 2.3 The transaction

```
BEGIN;  -- retried whole on deadlock (04 §4.5)
  1. companies FOR UPDATE                       -- trade on-account only (credit hold)
  2. orders FOR UPDATE; re-check status
  3. collection_slots / collection_bookings     -- only if the order ends up cancelled whole (booking → cancelled)
  4. shipments of the order FOR UPDATE; re-check packed quantities
  5. stock_allocations of the affected lines FOR UPDATE, ascending id; release the cancelled
     quantity, unpicked allocations first, then picked (a put-away task is raised for those),
     each source largest id first; deallocation movements through DeallocationService,
     which locks stock_levels in the 02 §11.1 order; reserved serials released
  6. order_lines.cancelled_base_qty += quantity; order_lines.allocated_base_qty −= quantity
  7. order status recomputed (§2.1 last bullet)
  8. credit hold reduced, or payments refund row (FOR UPDATE on the original payment)
  9. order_cancellations + order_cancellation_lines inserted
 10. credit note, if one is needed; its number taken last (02 §11.3)
COMMIT;
AFTER COMMIT: gateway refund; order.items_cancelled to the customer (the acknowledgement CCR reg. 32(4) requires); warehouse told of put-away tasks
```

- **Racing dispatch:** dispatch locks `orders` first too (04 §7.2), so the two serialise. The loser re-reads the order. A quantity that has since been packed or dispatched is refused (409), and the request is never partly applied.
- **No gateway call inside the transaction** (invariant 6).
- **Idempotent:** the request carries an `Idempotency-Key`, stored in `order_cancellations.client_token`. A retry returns the first result.
- **Whole-order consumer cancellation (05.4 §13.2) writes an `order_cancellations` row as well** (`kind = 'whole'`), so every cancellation has one record and one acknowledgement path. Its existing behaviour is otherwise unchanged.

### 2.4 Schema (amendment to 02 §8.3 and two new tables, signed off 2026-10-05)

```sql
-- order_lines is migrated: ALTER
ALTER TABLE order_lines ADD COLUMN cancelled_base_qty integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 0;
ALTER TABLE order_lines ADD CONSTRAINT order_lines_cancel_chk CHECK (
  cancelled_base_qty >= 0
  AND cancelled_base_qty % pack_base_units = 0
  AND dispatched_base_qty + cancelled_base_qty <= base_qty) NOT VALID;
ALTER TABLE order_lines VALIDATE CONSTRAINT order_lines_cancel_chk;

-- the picking screen's partial index must leave out cancelled quantity
CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY order_lines_outstanding_v2_idx ON order_lines (order_id)
  INCLUDE (sku_id, base_qty, dispatched_base_qty, cancelled_base_qty)
  WHERE dispatched_base_qty + cancelled_base_qty < base_qty;
-- after the code reads the new predicate, in a later migration:
DROP INDEX CONCURRENTLY order_lines_outstanding_idx;

CREATE TABLE order_cancellations (
  id                          bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY,
  public_id                   text        NOT NULL,
  order_id                    bigint      NOT NULL REFERENCES orders (id),
  kind                        text        NOT NULL,
  initiated_by                text        NOT NULL,
  actor_user_id               bigint      REFERENCES users (id),
  customer_notified_at        timestamptz NOT NULL,   -- when the customer told us (CCR reg. 32)
  reason_code                 text,
  reason_detail               text,
  cancelled_net_minor         bigint      NOT NULL,
  cancelled_tax_minor         bigint      NOT NULL,
  cancelled_gross_minor       bigint      NOT NULL,
  delivery_refund_net_minor   bigint      NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  delivery_refund_tax_minor   bigint      NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  credit_hold_reduction_minor bigint      NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  credit_note_id              bigint      REFERENCES credit_notes (id),
  refund_payment_id           bigint      REFERENCES payments (id),
  client_token                text,
  created_at                  timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),

  CONSTRAINT order_cancellations_public_id_uq UNIQUE (public_id),
  CONSTRAINT order_cancellations_kind_chk CHECK (kind IN ('partial','whole')),
  CONSTRAINT order_cancellations_initiated_chk CHECK (initiated_by IN ('customer','staff','system')),
  CONSTRAINT order_cancellations_actor_chk CHECK (initiated_by <> 'staff' OR actor_user_id IS NOT NULL),
  -- Q-X1: only staff may cancel below a break quantity, and always with a reason
  CONSTRAINT order_cancellations_override_chk CHECK (reason_code IS DISTINCT FROM 'below_break_override'
    OR (initiated_by = 'staff' AND reason_detail IS NOT NULL AND btrim(reason_detail) <> '')),
  CONSTRAINT order_cancellations_sum_chk CHECK (
    cancelled_gross_minor = cancelled_net_minor + cancelled_tax_minor
    AND cancelled_net_minor >= 0 AND cancelled_tax_minor >= 0
    AND delivery_refund_net_minor >= 0 AND delivery_refund_tax_minor >= 0
    AND credit_hold_reduction_minor >= 0)
);

CREATE INDEX order_cancellations_order_idx ON order_cancellations (order_id, created_at);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX order_cancellations_token_uq ON order_cancellations (order_id, client_token)
  WHERE client_token IS NOT NULL;

CREATE TABLE order_cancellation_lines (
  id                     bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY,
  order_cancellation_id  bigint  NOT NULL REFERENCES order_cancellations (id),
  order_line_id          bigint  NOT NULL REFERENCES order_lines (id),
  cancelled_pack_qty     integer NOT NULL,
  cancelled_base_qty     integer NOT NULL,
  line_net_minor         bigint  NOT NULL,
  line_tax_minor         bigint  NOT NULL,
  line_gross_minor       bigint  NOT NULL,

  CONSTRAINT order_cancellation_lines_uq UNIQUE (order_cancellation_id, order_line_id),
  CONSTRAINT order_cancellation_lines_qty_chk CHECK (cancelled_pack_qty > 0 AND cancelled_base_qty > 0),
  CONSTRAINT order_cancellation_lines_money_chk CHECK (
    line_gross_minor = line_net_minor + line_tax_minor
    AND line_net_minor >= 0 AND line_tax_minor >= 0)
);

CREATE INDEX order_cancellation_lines_line_idx ON order_cancellation_lines (order_line_id);

-- reject_row_mutation() exists (02 §25.1): cancellations are a record, never edited
CREATE TRIGGER order_cancellations_immutable BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE ON order_cancellations
  FOR EACH ROW EXECUTE FUNCTION reject_row_mutation();
CREATE TRIGGER order_cancellation_lines_immutable BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE ON order_cancellation_lines
  FOR EACH ROW EXECUTE FUNCTION reject_row_mutation();
```

- **`order_lines.base_qty` and the line's money never change.** The placed order still reprints identically (invariant 4). What was cancelled lives in `cancelled_base_qty` and the cancellation rows.
- `cancelled_base_qty % pack_base_units = 0` keeps cancellations in whole packs. The relation "cancellation line `cancelled_base_qty` = `cancelled_pack_qty` × the line's `pack_base_units`" crosses tables, so the service enforces it and tests assert it. So does "Σ cancellation lines per order line = `order_lines.cancelled_base_qty`", which is checked by the nightly reconciliation (02 §11.4).
- `orders` gains no column. The order's effective total is its placed total minus Σ `cancelled_gross_minor`, read from `order_cancellations_order_idx` (Q-X3).
- Every `VALIDATE` succeeds: existing lines have `cancelled_base_qty = 0`.

### 2.6 Notifications (05.12 addition)

`order.items_cancelled`: a partial cancellation was made. It goes to the customer (user, guest email, or the trade order's placing user plus invoice recipients). It lists what was cancelled, the refund or reduced amount, and when the refund will arrive. For a consumer it is the acknowledgement CCR reg. 32(4) requires. A cancellation that ends with the whole order cancelled sends the existing `order.cancelled` instead.

### 2.7 Tests

Break quantity: a trade customer leaving a line below `applied_break_qty` is refused, cancelling the whole line is allowed, staff override needs a reason (`order_cancellations_override_chk`), and a consumer is never refused. The cumulative-rounding sums are exact over 1,000 generated orders with mixed cancellations and shipments. Cancel 1 of 3 packs, then dispatch the rest: the invoices plus the cancellation equal the line total. A packed quantity is refused. Cancel racing dispatch: exactly one wins per quantity. A deadlock-retry fixture. Unpicked allocations are released first. Serials are released. A batch-tracked line releases the right batch. A card captured gets a partial refund row, and the payment becomes `part_refunded`. An unpaid BACS order gets no refund. On account, the hold is reduced and released at 0. A receipt is credited with no account movement. A consumer is never re-priced and gets no delivery refund while anything is kept. Cancelling the last remaining quantity cancels the order (nothing dispatched) or makes it `dispatched` (something dispatched). Idempotent retry. The `order_cancellations` rows are immutable. A guest can act only through a valid link. The lock order is asserted by generated-SQL inspection.
